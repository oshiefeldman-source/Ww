# Labor's Rate Rise: voiceover script

25 seconds. Record as one take in ElevenLabs (voice: Charlie), fast and punchy, with a short pause between lines.
The first line must start immediately, with no lead-in breath.

## Hook: pick the one that matches the RBA's decision on Tuesday 3 November

- **Hike:** Rates just went up again. That's five this year, and it's on Labor.
- **Hold:** Rates held, for now. Here's why it's still on Labor.
- **Before the decision:** Rates are likely going up tomorrow. Here's why it's on Labor.

## Then

One. They're spending big. Over a quarter of the whole economy.

Two. The Reserve Bank said it. Spending is one cause of inflation.

Three. So did the last governor, Philip Lowe.

Four. Inflation's going the wrong way. Four point three per cent.

Five. And Chalmers says spending isn't to blame.

Who's to blame? Comment Labor or RBA.

---

Beats: hook 3.0 s, reasons 3.4 s each, ending 4.2 s.
