# Five Ways Out (short): voiceover script

25-second cut. Record it as one take in ElevenLabs (voice: Charlie), with a short pause between lines.
Read it fast and punchy. The first line must start immediately, with no lead-in breath.

Albo could be gone, and you'd never get a vote.

One. He quits, and Labor picks the next PM.

Two. His own party dumps him.

Three. The House votes him out.

Four. The High Court rules him ineligible.

Five. The Governor-General sacks him. It happened in seventy-five.

Which one's most likely? Comment a number.

---

Timing: each line has to fit its beat (hook 3.0 s, routes 3.4 s, end 4.2 s).
Split the take into vo0–vo6.mp3 and run `./mix.sh`; lines that run slightly long are sped up (max 25%).
